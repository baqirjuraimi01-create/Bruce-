# Training & Nutrition Plan

25 y/o male, 4 years of lifting, returning to running, wants strength and mass
kept while building core strength and leg stability.

The same content is on the app's **Plan** tab so you can read it at the gym.
Every calorie figure below was calculated from the app's own food table, not
estimated.

## Part 1 — Nutrition

### Your plan as written is about 1,040 calories a day

This is the thing to fix before anything else. Adding your foods up exactly:

| Meal | kcal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Breakfast — 80 g oats, 1 scoop Greek yogurt, 1 scoop whey, honey | 543 | 44.6 g | 76.6 g | 7.4 g |
| Lunch — 100 g chicken | 165 | 31.0 g | 0 g | 3.6 g |
| Dinner — 200 g chicken | 330 | 62.0 g | 0 g | 7.2 g |
| **Total** | **1,038** | **137.6 g** | **76.6 g** | **18.2 g** |

Your protein estimate was close — 138 g, not 130. But you need roughly **2,700
kcal** at 75 kg with this training load, and you are about to add running on
top of it.

Eating at half your requirement while lifting five times per cycle does the
opposite of what you want: you lose muscle, strength stalls, sleep degrades,
and — at 18 g of fat a day, long-term — hormones follow. You asked how to add
20 g of protein. The protein was never the problem.

### The five fixes

**1. Stop putting 62 g of protein in one meal.** Move 50 g of chicken from
dinner to lunch. Four feedings of 35-40 g beats 45 / 31 / 62 for muscle
protein synthesis, and it keeps you full across the day instead of starving
until dinner.

**2. The missing 20 g is a pre-bed snack.** 200 g of Greek yogurt is ~20 g of
slow-digesting protein and covers the 9-hour overnight gap. Cottage cheese or
a casein scoop do the same job.

**3. Fat is at 18 g. It needs to be 60-75 g.** That is 0.8-1 g per kg. Two
whole eggs at breakfast, a thumb of olive oil on each cooked meal, a handful
of almonds, salmon twice a week. Nothing exotic.

**4. "Carbs tbc" is the running problem.** You are on 77 g of carbs a day, all
of it before 9am, and you want to run in the evenings. Put **300 g cooked
rice** at both lunch and dinner. Target 4-5 g per kg — about 300-375 g at
75 kg. Carbs are what make an easy run feel easy.

**5. Nothing in the plan is a vegetable.** 150 g of veg at lunch and dinner
plus a piece of fruit. You want 30 g+ of fibre daily; oats and chicken get you
nowhere near it, and it shows up in how you actually feel day to day.

Calories, carbs and fat all land where they should. Protein at 205 g is more
than you need — harmless, but it is the expensive part of the shop. Dropping
lunch and dinner chicken to 120 g each gives **2,659 kcal and 186 g protein**,
still comfortably clear of the ~150 g you need at 75 kg.

All five meals are one-tap presets on the app's Food tab.

Going from ~1,000 to ~2,700 kcal will feel like a lot of food, and the scale
will likely jump 1-2 kg in the first week — that is food volume, water and
glycogen, not fat. If it is uncomfortable, climb in two steps: lunch and dinner
carbs in week one, snacks in week two.

### Timing

- Proper meal 1.5-2 h before lifting. Training a squat session fasted wastes it.
- Easy runs are fine fasted. Interval days are not — have a banana first.
- 30-40 g of protein within a couple of hours after lifting. The 30-minute
  "anabolic window" is a myth; the daily total is what matters.

### Supplements worth the money

- **Creatine monohydrate, 5 g daily**, any time. Most proven supplement there
  is, and it helps the stability work too.
- **Vitamin D3, 1000-2000 IU** if you are indoors most of the day.
- **Salt and fluid** around runs. Most "bad runs" are dehydration.
- Skip BCAAs — at 150 g+ of protein they do nothing for you.

## Part 2 — Training

Your split, kept as you wrote it. Three changes:

1. **Rest days carry the running.** Four rest days and a running goal do not
   coexist. Three of them now have a session on them.
2. **Arms day picked up single-leg work.** You trained legs once every nine
   days — about three times a month — while asking for leg stability. Arms day
   is short, so it now opens with unilateral lower-body work. Legs get a heavy
   day and a stability day per cycle without adding a session.
3. **Every lifting day ends with core**, organised by what the core actually
   does: resist extension, resist rotation, then flex.

### The 9-day rotation

**Day 1 — Push** · chest, shoulders, triceps + anti-extension core
- Barbell Bench Press — 4 × 5-8
- Incline Dumbbell Press — 3 × 8-12
- Standing Overhead Press — 3 × 6-10 *(standing, not seated — the trunk work is the point)*
- Cable Lateral Raise — 3 × 12-15
- Overhead Cable Triceps Extension — 3 × 10-12
- Dips or Triceps Pushdown — 2 × 10-15
- **Ab Wheel Rollout — 3 × 8-12** *(core)*
- **Long-lever Plank — 3 × 30-45s** *(core)*
- Finish: 10-15 min easy walk

**Day 2 — Pull** · back, rear delts, biceps + anti-rotation core
- Weighted Pull-up / Lat Pulldown — 4 × 6-10
- Chest-supported Row — 4 × 8-12
- **Half-kneeling 1-arm Cable Row — 3 × 10-12/side** *(core)*
- Face Pull — 3 × 15
- Incline Dumbbell Curl — 3 × 10-12
- Hammer Curl — 2 × 12
- **Pallof Press — 3 × 12/side** *(core)*
- **Suitcase Carry — 3 × 30-40 m/side** *(core)*
- Finish: 15 min easy walk

**Day 3 — Rest / Easy Run A**
- Run 20-30 min, conversational pace. If you cannot talk in full sentences,
  slow down.
- Ankle Dorsiflexion Rock 2 × 10/side · 90/90 Hip Switch 2 × 8/side · Couch
  Stretch 2 × 45s/side
- *Not run in a while? Do 30-40 min brisk walking for two weeks, then run/walk
  intervals (2 min run / 2 min walk).*

**Day 4 — Legs** · bilateral strength + single-leg stability + ankle durability
- Back Squat — 4 × 4-6
- Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 6-8
- **Bulgarian Split Squat — 3 × 8-10/side** *(stability — the single best one here; 3s down, no wobble)*
- Single-leg Leg Curl / Nordic — 3 × 6-10 *(hamstring health is what keeps you running)*
- Standing Calf Raise — 4 × 8-12
- **Tibialis Raise — 3 × 15-20** *(stability — your shin-splint insurance)*
- **Copenhagen Plank — 2 × 20-30s/side** *(stability — adductors)*
- **Dead Bug — 3 × 8/side** *(core)*

**Day 5 — Rest / Run B + Balance**
- Weeks 1-4: easy 25-30 min. Week 5+: 6-8 × (1 min hard / 2 min easy) inside an
  easy run.
- **Single-leg Stance, eyes closed — 3 × 30s/side** *(barefoot; eyes closed forces the ankle to work)*
- **Single-leg RDL, bodyweight — 3 × 10/side**
- **Lateral Band Walk — 3 × 15/side**
- *One hard running day per cycle for the first 6 weeks. Two is how people get
  shin splints.*

**Day 6 — Upper** · higher-rep volume + hanging/lateral core
- Incline Bench Press — 3 × 8-12
- Pull-up — 3 × (2 short of failure)
- Seated Dumbbell Press — 3 × 10-12
- Cable Row — 3 × 12-15
- Lateral Raise — 3 × 15-20
- Rear Delt Fly — 3 × 15-20
- **Hanging Leg Raise — 3 × 10-15** *(core; no swinging — switch to knee raises if you swing)*
- **Side Plank with Reach — 3 × 30s/side** *(core)*

**Day 7 — Rest / Long Easy**
- 35-50 min easy the whole way. Walk breaks are fine and are not failure.
- Foam roll quads, calves, glutes — 8 min

**Day 8 — Arms + Lower Stability**
- **Slow Step-up, knee over toe — 3 × 8-10/side** *(3s up, 3s down, no push off the back foot)*
- **Reverse Lunge — 3 × 10/side**
- **Single-leg Calf Raise — 3 × 12-15/side**
- **Banded Ankle Eversion — 2 × 20/side** *(boring, 90 seconds, and it is why your ankles stop rolling)*
- EZ Bar Curl — 3 × 8-12
- Incline / Preacher Curl — 3 × 10-12
- Close-grip Bench or Dips — 3 × 8-12
- Cable Pushdown — 3 × 12-15
- **Weighted Plank — 3 × 30-45s** · **Hollow Hold — 3 × 20-30s** *(core)*

**Day 9 — Full Rest**
- Optional 20-30 min walk, 8 h sleep, hit your protein. Nothing else. This is
  where the adaptation actually happens.

### Progression

- **Double progression:** stay at the same weight until you hit the top of the
  rep range on every set, then add 2.5 kg upper / 5 kg lower.
- Main lifts: stop 1-2 reps short of failure. Accessories: 0-1 short.
- **Deload every 6th cycle:** same exercises, 2 sets each, ~60% of usual, no
  intervals.
- Running volume climbs no more than ~10% per week. The Cardio tab compares
  this week to last and tells you when you are over it.
- Joint pain (not muscle soreness) for 3 sessions running means swap the
  exercise, not push through.

### What to expect

Running will blunt your leg session for about four weeks. That is normal and it
passes. Hold squat weight where it is for the first two cycles rather than
fighting it — you are asking your body to adapt to a new stressor, and the
adaptation is the point.
